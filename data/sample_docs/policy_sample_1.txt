All users must authenticate with unique credentials before accessing the production environment.

Security-relevant events, including failed logins and privilege changes, must be logged and monitored.

Customer data is retained for seven years unless legal or contractual obligations require a longer period.

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within 24 hours.

Customer data at rest must be encrypted using industry-standard algorithms such as AES-256.

Third-party vendors must undergo a security review before onboarding.

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company systems.
